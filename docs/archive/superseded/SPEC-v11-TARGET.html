Where the shipped app breaks on a real situation

Each row is a thing the current app cannot represent, discovered by trying to model an actual multi-rate agency worker with a dated foreign-currency obligation and a 0% promo running out. This is what makes the spec evidence-based rather than a wish list.

₦3m with a hard date		 Currency is tied to the tax model. One currency, full stop. The single largest obligation cannot be entered at all. Parked 6 Jul.

"Should I take this shift?" Shows total hours needed, not hours beyond what is already committed. The user does the subtraction by hand — which is the job the app exists to do.

Not in v11, and why

Accounts, sync, any backend

Gated on Q1 returning a positive answer.

  1. The gate is 20 real users with week-two return.
  2. Building it before the gate answers a question nobody has asked.
  3. It also converts a localStorage tool into a data controller with a breach surface.

Cookieless analytics

Cut deliberately, 18 Aug 2026.

  1. Answers part of one of the four questions and none of the other three.
  2. Below ~100 users it is ceremony.
  3. Re-add when volume justifies the privacy-notice surface.

Live FX rates

Rejected on the offline-first constraint.

  1. Core loop must run with no network. An FX call breaks that.
  2. User-entered rate + date stamp + staleness warning is honest and offline.
  3. Revisit only if a backend exists — which is itself gated.

Any ranking of obligations

Permanently out. Not a version decision.

  1. "Pay this first" is debt counselling under art. 39E RAO / PERG 17.
  2. Stating a shortfall is fine. Suggesting what to do about it is not.
  3. Must be in ARCHITECTURE.md with the reason, so a future session cannot helpfully improve the app into a regulated activity.
Legal readings are sourced, not advised — §3.6's debt-counselling reading sits at 65% confidence and is unreviewed by a solicitor. Tax models remain simplified effective-rate estimates.
